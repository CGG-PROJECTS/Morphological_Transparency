Morphological Transparency Norms

Project Summary

This dataset provides large-scale estimates of semantic transparency and perceived morphological structure for English polymorphemic words.

  • Study 1: Semantic distance between a derivation and its baseword (Nagy & Anderson, 1984) generated via GPT-4.1-mini.
  • Study 2: Crowdsourced human norms and fine-tuned LLM estimates of how many morphemes readers typically perceive in polymorphemic words.

You can search and sort individual words below, or use the instructions to import the raw data into Python or R.
