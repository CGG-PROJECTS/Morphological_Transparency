Morphological Transparency Norms Dashboard

Project Summary

This dataset provides large-scale estimates of semantic transparency and perceived morphological structure for English polymorphemic words.

  • Study 1: Semantic distance between a derivation and its baseword (Nagy & Anderson, 1984).
  • Study 2: LLM and crowdsourced estimates of perceived morphemes and discrepancy from ELP-listed counts.

Understanding the Metrics

Study 1: Semantic Distance Scale

Ratings 0–2 are treated as semantically transparent; 3–5 as semantically opaque.

  • SEM 0: Semantically transparent.
  • SEM 1: Meaning inferable from baseword with minimal context.
  • SEM 2: Meaning inferable with moderate context.
  • SEM 3: Requires substantial context.
  • SEM 4: Distant relation; hard to infer from base.
  • SEM 5: No clear semantic connection.

Study 2: Morpheme Count Discrepancy

  • Signed discrepancy: Perceived minus ELP count (negative = undersegmentation; positive = oversegmentation).
  • Absolute discrepancy: Mismatch size; 0 = transparent match.
  • Transparency proximity (0–1): 1 = perfect alignment.
  • Child_Database_Sources: Indicates where a word occurs in child/school-related print.

Child_Database_Sources legend

  • COCA_Juv_Fiction: Juvenile fiction subset of COCA (Davies, 2008–).
  • CYP_LEX: Children and Young People’s Books Lexicon (Korochkina et al., 2024).
  • CPB_Lex: Children’s Picture Books Lexicon (Green et al., 2024).
  • Core_Reading_Programs: Vocabulary in major US elementary reading programs (Graves et al., 2019).
  • Living_Word_Vocab / VXGL: Grade-level vocabulary resources.
